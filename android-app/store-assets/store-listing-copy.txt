--------------------------------------------------------
SKIL Master is the dedicated business intelligence, accounting synchronization, and receivables management application for SKIL Lifestyle LLP (Rayagada, Odisha).

Designed for partners, accounting teams, and authorized field personnel, SKIL Master brings TallyPrime ERP data, supplier scheme tracking, and customer collection workflows together in one mobile workspace.

KEY FEATURES

• Financial Overview Dashboard
Track monthly and cumulative sales turnover, purchase COGS, gross margin, supplier scheme claims, and net realized profit alongside visual income-versus-expense and liability-ratio analytics.

• Supplier Intelligence Hubs
Monitor brand-wise procurement, dealership targets, shortfalls, and invoice matching for authorized footwear suppliers including Ajanta Shoes and Xpania Footcare, with instant PDF invoice vault access.

• Customer Ledger Aging & Settlement
View party-wise outstanding balances across aging brackets (0–30, 31–45, 46–60, and 60+ days), track eligible cash discount (CD) windows, and prepare accurate bill-wise settlement summaries.

• Accounting Reconciliation & Audit
Verify physical supplier PDFs against Tally vouchers, check bank statement reconciliation variances, and audit supplier scheme credit notes.

• Inventory & Ledger Lookup
Inspect item-level stock movement and search party ledgers and vouchers across Sales, Purchase, Receipt, Payment, and Credit Note registers.

• Offline-Ready & Encrypted Cloud Sync
Sync over local Wi-Fi or encrypted cloud snapshots with AES-256 data protection and offline cached availability.

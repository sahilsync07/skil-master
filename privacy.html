Privacy Policy for SKIL Master

Entity: SKIL LIFESTYLE LLP | Effective Date: 11-November-2026

1. Overview & Entity Information

This Privacy Policy describes how SKIL LIFESTYLE LLP ("we", "our", or "us"), a limited liability partnership registered under the laws of India with its principal office at Rayagada, Odisha, India, manages, protects, and handles information within the mobile application SKIL Master (Package Identifier: com.skillifestyle.master).

SKIL Master is an internal business operations, inventory audit, accounts receivable, and trade reconciliation platform designed exclusively for the partners, authorized personnel, and trade distributors of SKIL Lifestyle LLP.

2. Information We Collect

The application is tailored for commercial trade utility and strictly minimizes data collection:

  • Commercial & Transactional Data: The application processes wholesale invoices, credit notes, customer ledger balances, and payment status sourced from authorized enterprise accounting systems (TallyPrime).
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Basic device telemetry (device model, operating system version, crash diagnostics, and network status) necessary to maintain secure synchronization and operational stability.
  • Permissions: The application may request access to internet connectivity for synchronization, and local storage access solely for downloading authorized accounting summaries, invoices, or statements.

🚫 We do NOT collect personal sensitive data such as biometric identifiers, contacts, precise GPS location, photos, microphone audio, or consumer banking credentials.

3. How Information Is Used

All information processed by SKIL Master is utilized strictly for legitimate internal enterprise purposes:

  • Facilitating wholesale customer settlement calculations and prompt payment cash discount (CD) reminders.
  • Reconciling supplier dispatches and claims with footwear manufacturers (including Ajanta Shoes India Pvt. Ltd. and Xpania Footcare LLP).
  • Ensuring GST statutory compliance and sales register verification.
  • Monitoring internal inventory stock and logistics movements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Zero Commercial Data Monetization:

• We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize any commercial or user data to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketers.

• The application contains NO advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no third-party profiling tools.

• Data is shared strictly with regulatory or tax authorities only where legally mandated by Indian law (such as GST audits).

5. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard cryptographic practices (including HTTPS/TLS encrypted transmissions and restricted local device sandboxing) to protect your business records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Business records are retained only for the duration required by statutory taxation and commercial laws of India.

6. Children's Privacy

SKIL Master is exclusively intended for adult business partners, operations staff, and commercial traders aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 18.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory modifications or system enhancements. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.

## 4. Critical Audit Observations & Corrections Required Before Filing

### 1. Tax Ledger Correction on Sales Bill #SKIL-058 (Action Required)
* **Voucher:** `SKIL-058` dt 21-10-2026 | Party: **Famous Foot Wear** (Vizianagaram, AP)
* **Error:** The voucher has ledger entries:
  - `GST Sales`: ₹61,932.00 (Taxable)
  - `Output SGST`: ₹1,548.30
  - ⚠️ `Input CGST`: ₹1,548.30 *(Wrong ledger!)*
  - `Round Off -(+/-)`: ₹0.40
  - `Famous Foot Wear`: ₹65,029.00
* **Impact:** Because it is an outward sales invoice, using `Input CGST` will cause GSTR-1 to omit the ₹1,548.30 Central Tax, and falsely inflate ITC in GSTR-3B.
* **Fix:** Open voucher `SKIL-058` in Tally, change `Input CGST` to **`Output CGST`** (or `Output IGST` @ 5% if interstate). 

### 2. Stock Item Name Typo in Bill #SKIL-056 (Cosmetic Correction)
* **Voucher:** `SKIL-056` dt 10-10-2026 | Party: **M/s. Rajan Traders**
* **Observation:** Item named `XP 12A4 PISTA 05/09 MRP479/-` was billed at rate ₹226.00.
* **Audit Finding:** On Xpania's original inward supplier invoice `XPL/2366/26-27`, item `12A4 PISTA 5/9` has MRP **₹389.00** and rate **₹226.00**. At MRP 389, ₹226 is exactly **41.90% (~42%)**, which is 100% consistent with the rest of the bill. The item name in Tally was simply created with a typo (`MRP479/-` instead of `MRP389/-`).
* **Fix:** Rename the stock item in Tally to `XP 12A4 PISTA 05/09 MRP389/-`.

### 3. Rate Anomaly on Ajanta Item in Bill #SKIL-002
* **Voucher:** `SKIL-002` dt 12-08-2026 | Party: **Delight Foot Wear**
* **Observation:** Item `AJ CG 40 SKY 07/10 MRP319/-` was billed at rate ₹276.19 (taxable for MRP 290) instead of ₹303.81 (taxable for MRP 319).
* **Audit Finding:** On Ajanta's purchase invoice `1900402722`, Ajanta printed MRP 290.00 while charging ₹184.37. SKIL billed the customer based on printed MRP 290. Customer got 37.5% on 290.
